# AI safety and research boundaries

PamatiAI performs sentiment/affect analysis and support-oriented risk signaling, NOT clinical diagnosis. It must never determine whether a student has a psychiatric disorder, prescribe treatment, or replace counselors, psychologists, psychiatrists, physicians or emergency services. AI risk indicators require authorized human oversight.

No inference is active in this foundation. The AI package defines contracts only. Never substitute random scores, keyword heuristics or fabricated confidence values to make a portal appear complete.

Future analysis requires informed, purpose-specific consent, optional independent modality choices and withdrawal without coercion. Provide text-only and non-AI support paths. Visual affect signals are uncertain and culturally/contextually dependent; facial appearance must not be treated as reliable evidence of mental state. Minimize raw media, abstain for unavailable or unsupported inputs and disclose model limitations.

Support replies must avoid clinical claims, encourage human support when appropriate and provide institution-verified local emergency resources when urgent help is requested. Do not invent local contact details. Signals are reviewed conservatively by trained humans; escalation timing, staffing, legal basis and appeals must be approved institutionally. No autonomous emergency dispatch, disciplinary decision or diagnosis.

Evaluate false positives and false negatives, uncertainty calibration, bias across language/dialect and demographic groups, accessibility and longitudinal stability. Keep reproducible model revisions and held-out evaluation datasets; prevent student leakage across splits. Record human review outcomes without treating them as clinical ground truth. Prompt injection must not change access controls or consent checks. Models have no direct authority to contact third parties or fetch student records.
